# Decision Ledger

Use one entry per consequential decision.

## Template

### ADR-MEM-000 — Decision title

- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded
- Context: why a decision was required
- Decision: what was chosen
- Rationale: why this option was selected
- Consequences: benefits, costs, and follow-up work
- Evidence: links, tests, or source files
