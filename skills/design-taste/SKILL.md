# Design Taste

Tune three project dials from 1 to 10:

- design variance
- motion intensity
- visual density

Require a visual thesis before implementation. Reject arbitrary gradients, uniform card grids, decorative noise, excessive rounded containers, weak typography, and motion without purpose. Review desktop and mobile screenshots against hierarchy, rhythm, affordance, contrast, consistency, and brand distinction.

The optional upstream Taste Skill may be installed through `npx skills add https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill`. Keep attribution and MIT license notices for copied or modified upstream content.
